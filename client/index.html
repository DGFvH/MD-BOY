What is Hashlite?

Hashlite is a free online Markdown editor and viewer. Write or paste Markdown and see it formatted as you type, then export it as a PDF, HTML or Markdown file, or copy it straight into Word, Google Docs or an email. It works without an account; a free account adds folders, search and version history.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The editor on the home page works right away: write, preview, open files and export without signing up. Your text is kept in your browser. A free account adds autosave to the cloud, folders, search, version history, images and share links.

Can I paste Markdown from an AI assistant?

Yes. AI chat assistants usually answer in Markdown. Paste the answer into the editor to see the headings, lists, tables, code and math formatted, fix what you need, and export it as a PDF or copy it into Word or Google Docs.

How do I get Markdown into Word or Google Docs?

Open the ⋯ menu and choose Copy as formatted text, then paste into Word, Google Docs or an email. Headings, lists, tables, links and code keep their formatting.

Can I export to PDF or HTML?

Yes. From the ⋯ menu, choose Print / Save as PDF, or download the document as a standalone .html or as .md.

Is Hashlite free?

Yes. Hashlite is free, has no ads and is open source.

Does it support math and diagrams?

Yes. LaTeX math between $ or $$ is rendered with KaTeX, and mermaid code blocks are drawn as diagrams.

Can a link open Markdown directly in the editor?

Yes. Link to %PUBLIC_URL%/#text= followed by the URL-encoded Markdown. The text is read from the part after #, so it is never sent to a server.

Is my data private?

Without an account, your text never leaves your browser. With an account, documents are stored in the EU and are private unless you create a read-only share link. There are no ads, and analytics cookies are only set if you allow them.
