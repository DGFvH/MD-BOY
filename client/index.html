What is Hashmark?

Hashmark is a free online Markdown editor that shows a live preview as you type and saves your documents automatically to your account. It runs in any modern browser on desktop and phone, supports GitHub-flavoured Markdown with math and diagrams, and exports to Markdown, HTML, PDF or a .zip of everything.

It is built for notes, documentation, READMEs, meeting minutes and anything else you would rather write in plain text. Hashmark is open source and can be self-hosted with Node.js and SQLite.

Everything you need to write in Markdown

A focused editor with the features you actually use, and nothing in your way.

  • Live preview

    Split view with synced scrolling, or switch to editor-only or preview-only. Toolbar and keyboard shortcuts included.

  • Autosave you can trust

    Every change is saved to the server automatically, with a local draft in your browser so nothing is lost if you go offline.

  • Version history

    Automatic and manual versions you can preview and restore. Editing in two tabs or devices? Both versions are kept.

  • Folders, search and trash

    Organise documents in folders with drag and drop, find anything with full-text search, and restore from the trash.

  • GitHub-flavoured Markdown

    Tables, task lists you can tick in the preview, footnotes, autolinks, callouts, ==highlights==, :emoji: and YAML front matter.

  • Math, diagrams and code

    KaTeX math, Mermaid diagrams and syntax highlighting for code blocks, all rendered live in the preview.

  • Images made easy

    Paste, drop or upload an image and Hashmark inserts the Markdown for you.

  • Import, export and share

    Import .md files. Export as .md, standalone offline HTML or PDF, download everything as .zip, or share a read-only link.

  • Light, dark and accessible

    Light and dark themes, fully keyboard accessible, and comfortable on a phone as well as a large screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hashmark free?

Yes. Hashmark is free to use and has no ads. It is also open source, so you can run your own copy.

Do I need an account?

Yes, you sign up with an email address and a password. The account is what lets Hashmark save your documents and give you the same documents on every device.

Where are my documents stored?

Documents are saved automatically to the Hashmark server as you type. Your browser also keeps a local draft, so an edit is not lost if the connection drops. Version history keeps earlier versions you can preview and restore.

Can I export to PDF or HTML?

Yes. You can export a document as a .md file or as a standalone HTML file that works offline, print it or save it as PDF from the print dialog, and download all your documents at once as a .zip.

Does it support math and diagrams?

Yes. Write LaTeX math between $ or $$ and it is rendered with KaTeX. Write a Mermaid diagram in a mermaid code block and it is drawn in the preview. Tables, task lists, footnotes, callouts and syntax highlighting are supported too.

Can I use it offline?

Partly. If your connection drops while you write, Hashmark keeps a local draft in your browser and saves it to the server when you are back online. Opening the app and signing in needs a connection.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. Hashmark is open source and runs on Node.js with SQLite. You can run it directly or with Docker on your own server.

Is my data private?

Your documents are private to your account unless you create a read-only share link. Hashmark uses one session cookie to keep you signed in, and has no tracking, no analytics and no ads. You can export everything or delete your account at any time.

Chatting with AI models about your documents is planned for a later version. See the privacy page for exactly what is stored.
